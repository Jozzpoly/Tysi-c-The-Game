6. CI captured source, pending, accepted, resolving, value-transfer and settled frames separately.

## Visual/causal review

### Pending

Improved.

The committed card is visibly separated from the private hand and occupies a provisional relation with the shared trick while authority remains unresolved.

This supports the `locally immediate / globally honest` timing model better than v1.

### Accepted / trick closure

Improved.

The three card identities coexist long enough to read as one closed trick before collection.

The scene now has a clearer causal middle rather than jumping directly from hand to final value.

### Resolving / collection

Directionally improved but still weak.

The trick leaves the shared scene toward captured ownership, but the destination currently reads too much like a right-side information rail, especially on desktop.

This exposes a geography problem rather than an animation problem.

### Captured-value consequence

Improved.

The `+25` transient and `65` stable value are now explicitly associated with a captured-card pile and remain separate from match score `340`.

However, the captured region still needs stronger spatial ownership by the local player.

### Settled state

Improved but not sufficient.

- central explanatory text fades;
- local hand becomes the next initiative locus;
- captured pile/value retains a recent-consequence residue.

This is closer to the target `consequence -> settle -> next action` grammar.

The scene still does not yet have enough character to justify Owner perception testing.

## Major new finding — platform geometry is not just responsive scaling

The same semantic layout behaves materially differently across mobile and desktop.

### Mobile

The composition is relatively coherent:

- private hand occupies a large fraction of useful width;
- opponent/source regions remain perceptually near the shared scene;
- hand, trick and captured value can be scanned with modest gaze travel.

This does not certify mobile quality, but the spatial relationships survive reasonably well.

### Desktop

The same geometry becomes excessively sparse:

- the private hand is visually too small for its intended importance;
- opponent seats are pushed toward remote screen corners;
- source-to-trick paths become long and visually weak;
- captured ownership on the right reads more like HUD than local player territory;
- large negative space becomes deadness rather than useful quiet.

This is direct evidence for the Desktop Body / Composition track:

> semantic parity does not imply geometric parity.

Desktop needs a deliberately denser causal geography and a stronger private-workspace scale, not an enlarged mobile canvas with more empty space.

## Opponent source replay finding

Mechanically, source replay is correct and privacy-safe.

Perceptually, current source staging is too weak in static temporal capture, especially on desktop.

Likely causes:

- actor anchors are too far from shared action;
- the source ghost travels through low-information empty space;
- source emphasis is too subtle relative to travel distance.

Do **not** fix this first with glow/trails/personality animation.

First correct actor geography.

## Hand finding

The private hand remains the strongest region of the fixture, but desktop under-scales it.

The local-field response exists during manipulation, yet the resting composition still does not communicate much personal topology beyond sequence/order.

This is acceptable for the current slice, but not a final Hand Workspace solution.

## Updated spatial hypothesis

For the local player:

- private hand;
- captured-card/value ownership;
- next initiative;

should likely share a recognizable **local actor territory** rather than being split across unrelated screen regions.

This may let trick collection return perceptually toward the winner without building a permanent scoreboard/panel system.

For opponents, the same principle can later map public ownership/initiative toward their seat territories.

## V2 verdict

V2 is a real improvement over v1 because it demonstrates more of the causal sentence and exposes platform-specific spatial failure.

It still does not deserve Owner testing.

Most important new result:

> living interaction requires causal geography at the scale of the body/platform, not only correct semantic anchors and animation order.
