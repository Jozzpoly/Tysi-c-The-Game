# Experience research

This directory contains non-authoritative research contracts for the presentation / interaction track of **Tysiąc The Game**.

Canonical gameplay truth remains in core state, legal commands, reducer events and viewer-safe projections. These documents define how we investigate the experience built on top of that truth.

## Campaign / method

- `EXPERIENCE_RESEARCH_CHARTER.md` — evidence model, direct-manipulation principles, truth boundaries and cross-project research standard.
- `EXPERIENCE_CAMPAIGN_MAP.md` — multi-run roadmap from card embodiment through opponent presence, self-teaching UX, stakes, sensory language, platform embodiment and torture/integration.
- `OWNER_EXPERIENCE_TEST_PROTOCOL.md` — how raw Owner experience evidence is captured without forcing design jargon.

## Current foundation run

- `RUN_01_CARD_OBJECT_AND_HAND.md` — tactile card object, private hand territory, manual ordering, safe probing, play/reorder gestures, latency/rejection and torture cases.
- `SELF_TEACHING_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md` — teaching routine interaction through affordance, direct manipulation, constraint and causal consequence instead of banners and arrows.

A richer standalone Card/Hand embodiment fixture is currently being iterated locally before it is promoted into the branch. It explores direct grabbing, horizontal manual reorder, upward play intent, material legal/illegal constraint, simulated latency/rejection, local sound/haptic reinforcement and new-card insertion without auto-resorting the player's hand.

## Lab fixtures

Standalone fixtures live under `lab/experience/`. They are deliberately cheap to reject and must not be mistaken for production UI or rule authority.

Static screenshots can evaluate composition. They do **not** prove feel, timing, touch quality or repeated-use fatigue.
